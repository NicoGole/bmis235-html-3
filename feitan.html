Backstory

Feitan never knew his parents. He was left as a baby in the back alleys of a crowded port city, and because he was a tiefling, most people crossed the street rather than help him. He learned early that the safest thing to be was unseen.

He grew up picking pockets, sleeping on rooftops, and listening. A quiet kid with a knack for disappearing was useful to the wrong people, and by the time he was grown, Feitan had become a professional: a hired shadow who could get into any room and out again before anyone noticed.

That skill is how he met the troupe. He was leaning against a tavern wall, listening to Mogneuss and Bjorn plan something dangerous in whispers, and decided it sounded more interesting than anything he had been paid to do. He has been around ever since, though he will never admit he chose to stay.

Personality

Feitan is calm, quiet, and nonchalant to a fault. Ambushes, curses, and hands bursting out of the ground barely earn a raised eyebrow. He speaks only when he has something worth saying, which is not often. The two things that crack his cool are a good bet and his own tail, which wags or stiffens and gives away exactly what his face will not.

Abilities

  • Assassinate: acts first in a fight and strikes with advantage against anyone who has not moved yet, dealing extra damage in the first round.
  • Sneak Attack: deals huge extra damage when he has advantage or an ally is next to his target.
  • Cunning Action: can dash, hide, or slip away as a bonus action every turn.
  • Steady Aim: holds perfectly still to line up a guaranteed advantage.
  • Uncanny Dodge: cuts the damage of an incoming hit in half.
  • Cunning Strike: trades some Sneak Attack damage to trip, poison, or disarm his target.
  • Assassin's Tools: a master of disguises and poisons.

The Poison Knife

On their travels, the troupe found a slim black dagger with a thin groove running down the blade, always slick with a faint green venom that never seems to dry. Feitan claimed it before anyone else could reach for it. In his hands, a single quiet cut is all it takes, and he likes it that way.
