Cassian Vantrell

Wood Elf Paladin · Oath of Vengeance

Who He Is

Cassian is a lean wood elf in his late twenties with sharp, angular features, glowing gold eyes, a small gold hoop in one ear, and a scar through his right eyebrow. He wears dark studded leather, an oxblood red scarf, and a gold sun-shaped pendant, with a rapier always at his side.

He is cool, confident, and almost never impressed. Even locked in a prison cell, he looked like he had been expecting someone to show up.

How He Joined

The troupe found Cassian in a prison run by the Dragonborn cult, where dozens of paladins were being held captive. Freeing him cost us dearly: our friend Bjorn, a human paladin who had been with us since the first session, fell in the fight against the cult superior Jameis. Cassian walked out of his cell into a battle and has fought beside us ever since.

His Oath

Cassian follows the Oath of Vengeance, a vow to punish the wicked and fight the greatest evil, whatever it takes. In the colosseum he faced Azrakar, a paladin who broke his own oath, and the two argued about what an oath really means while their blades were locked together.

Greatest Moment

At the end of the Festival of the Moon battle, Cassian landed the final blow on the cult commander Razehar. The fight was meant to be for sport, so he knocked Razehar down instead of killing him, while the shocked crowd watched in silence.
