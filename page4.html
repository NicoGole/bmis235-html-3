Musa

Druid · Circle of the Stars

Who He Is

Musa is the happy heart of the troupe. Cheerful, warm, and usually laughing, he balances out a party full of people who refuse to show their feelings. He carries a small pet rat on his shoulder and draws his magic from the constellations.

At the very first session, while everyone else was being mysterious, Musa was a few feet away happily spilling his drink all over himself.

Home: Moon Hallow

Musa grew up in Moon Hallow, a glowing forest town full of waterfalls, lanterns, and blossoming trees. When the troupe arrived, he was practically jumping for joy. There he reunited with Uncle Worm, a huge, green-skinned old man who proudly showed us a newspaper calling us the Phantom Troupe.

At the temple of W.R., Musa also ran into his old high school crush, Beatrice. After a hug and some very flustered conversation, she handed him a sending stone with a wink. The rest of us will never let him forget it.

Abilities

  • Starry Form: his body blazes with starlight and a glowing constellation appears behind him.
  • Archer Form: fires arrows made of pure starlight.
  • Healing Spirit: summons a glowing spirit deer that heals his friends.
  • Entangle: traps enemies in vines, like he did to Jameis in the prison.

Greatest Moment

In the colosseum, Musa was cut off alone with Razehar, a Dragonborn druid and cult commander who transformed into a dragon wyrmling. Musa held his ground in Starry Form, dodging claws and firing starlight arrows with his spirit deer healing him at his side.
